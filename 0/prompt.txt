# Owner's decisions (final)
1. A transfer with one side showing so far: the total is left as is, it's marked unresolved, and earlier days correct themselves when the other side lands. Never assume the other side.
2. Two balances disagreeing on the same date: use the most recently recorded one and tell the owner.
3. Cash machine withdrawal: a card offers to add it to the Cash account; until accepted it counts as money that left. After spending, the owner updates the balance. Cash in a Cash account counts in net worth.
4. A new bank or hand-added item in its first month: its value COUNTS in the net worth total, but the monthly change shows it as its own "newly added" part instead of a gain. The change and percent shown as the month's movement exclude it.
5. Archiving an account: it stays in past days up to the archive date, not removed from all history. If the account still has money, Moneo must ASK what happens to it before archiving: moved to another of the owner's accounts, taken out as cash, or gone.
