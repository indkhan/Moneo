## Decisions I made

- **Repeats:** resolved by rejecting through the normal review, so they stay audited and undoable.
- **Checks:** worked out when read rather than stored, so each piece of evidence is one entry that clears itself.
- **Negative Scalable cash:** kept as zero cash plus a debt.
- **Debt with no plan:** money owed with no repayment plan no longer stops the forecast.
- **Conflicting balances:** the strict "ambiguous" tie is gone, as decision 2 needed. Existing tests changed to match.
- **Bank-row proof:** the bank row carrying the same note as the deposit (8+ characters) also counts as wording proof.
- **Cash cards:** only for withdrawals from the last 30 days, and they aren't raised again once answered.
- **Repayments:** accepted oldest first, because a loan's value can't step back in time. A newer one first refuses the older one with a message.
- **Restoring an archived account:** its closing rows stay; each can be undone from its transaction.
- **Calculated-history check:** allows 10% or €20 between the last calculated day and the first real one.
