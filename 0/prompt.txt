## Two layout bugs on the Ask/chat page (`/ai`, `components/chat-workspace.tsx` and the app shell/layout around it)

Found by a QA audit; nothing in these files changed since. Fix both in this one PR.

### B1 — a populated chat scrolls the whole app off screen
Repro: open a conversation with many messages (audit: 42 messages, 65 conversations in the sidebar), click "Older conversations" in the sidebar until the list ends, then keep scrolling down with the wheel over the sidebar after it reaches its bottom. The scroll chains to the outer document: `document.scrollingElement.scrollTop` went from 0 to 7,637 px (1440×900), 9,559 (1024×500) and 14,014 (390×844); the app moved entirely above the viewport and the visible area was the bare HTML background. A new empty chat stayed at 0. A temporary `overscroll-behavior: contain` on the sidebar stopped the chaining, but the document was still taller than the viewport — so there are two causes to fix at the source:
1. The document itself is taller than the viewport on `/ai` (find which element overflows the app shell — likely the populated message pane or the sidebar not being constrained to the shell's height — and make the shell own the height so only the inner panels scroll; the page should never get document-level scroll on this route).
2. Scroll chaining from inner scrollers (sidebar `aside` at ~line 243, the message pane at ~258, the mobile conversations menu at ~249) — add `overscroll-contain` where an inner panel scrolls.
Check the other app pages share the same shell, and don't break pages that legitimately scroll the document (check Home, Transactions, Settings on desktop and mobile still scroll normally).

### B3 — a fresh chat's text box is unreachable on a short desktop window
Repro: at 1024×500, open New chat. The textarea sat at y=131–179 px while its visible scroll pane started at y=190 px, so clicking where it should be couldn't focus it; attachment and send were only partly visible. Present before any scrolling. Make the composer always fully visible and clickable on short viewports (e.g. the empty-chat intro/suggestions should scroll or shrink, not the composer).
