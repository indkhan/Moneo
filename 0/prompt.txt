One "yes" on an own-account card now remembers that account. It marks every matching transaction, past and future, as an own transfer and doesn't ask again.

- **What it saves:** the account goes on the Accounts page under the accounts Moneo doesn't track. It's saved by IBAN, or by the name on the payment when the bank gave no IBAN.
- **What it marks:** imports and bank sync mark later payments automatically. A name match also needs the name in the payment text and no other IBAN on the row, so a refund that only carries your name is left alone. The other open "your own account?" cards for the same account are closed.
- **Undo:** undoing any one of those transfers in the transaction drawer reverts all the transactions the confirmation reclassified and forgets the account. Accounts you add by hand still undo one transaction at a time.
- **Chat and voice:** both use the same path as the card, so they behave the same.
- **Migration:** `202611160001_confirmed_own_accounts.sql` makes the IBAN optional and adds a name key and a "confirmed" flag. It uses no extensions, so it runs on the desktop app's Postgres.
