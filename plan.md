# Personal Finance Workspace — Product Plan

## Purpose

A desktop-first website to **understand your money, plan decisions, and create reusable financial tools with AI**. Built for your personal use first, with separate private workspaces for other users later. Start with **CSV/Excel imports and manual entries**; real bank-account connections are part of the final product.

## Main application

| Area | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your financial overview: balances, net worth, estimated available-to-spend, spending, upcoming payments, goals, and important insights. Customizable dashboards combine built-in widgets and saved AI tools. No separate Money Overview. |
| **Money** | Manage accounts, transactions, recurring payments, investments, assets, and debt. Search, filter, save views, bulk-edit, categorize, tag, and group spending into events such as trips. Review uncertain imports and classifications. |
| **Plan** | Manage goals, virtual savings allocations, spending plans, budgets, forecasts, and what-if scenarios. One **Financial Model** area contains the facts, assumptions, and rules used by the application, with sources and editing controls. |
| **AI** | Persistent conversations, thorough financial reviews, saved analyses, a custom-tool library, and activity history. The same contextual AI side panel is available throughout the app. |

## Core experience

Import your data, review anything uncertain, and receive an initial financial analysis and personalized dashboard. Ask questions, investigate changes, compare decisions, and receive selective, goal-relevant insights. Saved reviews and optional scheduled summaries help track changes over time. The core screens remain usable without chatting.

## AI-generated tools

AI can create interactive planners, trackers, reports, and comparisons **when they add value—not for every complex question**. Tools use updated financial data, remember their settings, support AI and direct code editing, and have version history. Pin a useful widget, summary, or opening shortcut to a dashboard; a complete miniature interface is not mandatory. Keep PDF/image exports.

## Trust and control

Financial calculations come from application code, not AI guesses. Forecasts expose assumptions and missing information. Preserve original records, show evidence, propagate corrections, and provide history and undo. AI activity is visible and stoppable; data access is controllable. Keep straightforward AI preferences, model/provider settings, and usage visibility.

## Outside the current plan

Public artifact sharing, a full per-capability prompt-management interface, and external financial actions such as payments, transfers, trading, or subscription cancellation. **Bank connections bring data in; they do not authorize moving money.**
