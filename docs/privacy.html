What Moneo is

Moneo is open-source personal finance software that you install and run on your own computer. There is no Moneo service, account or server run by the developer.

Your data stays with you

Everything Moneo stores (accounts, transactions, imported statements, plans, chats and settings) is kept in a database on the computer where you run it. The developer of Moneo receives none of it: no analytics, tracking, telemetry or copies of your data.

Services you choose to connect

Moneo only contacts outside services that you set up yourself, and only sends what each feature needs:

  • Bank connections go through Enable Banking and your bank, under the consent you give your bank. Moneo reads balances and booked transactions for the accounts you approve.
  • Scalable Capital, if connected, shares your holdings, cash and portfolio value through its read-only access.
  • AI features send your questions and the parts of your data you allow (in Settings → AI and your data) to the provider you set up: OpenAI, through your ChatGPT plan or API key, or OpenRouter.

Each of these services handles data under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Settings → Reset all data empties your workspace and ends your bank connections. Removing Moneo's Docker volumes deletes the database entirely.
