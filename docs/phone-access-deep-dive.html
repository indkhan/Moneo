In use

You ask on the phone. The laptop does the work.

Your sign-in lives on the computer, so nothing about your AI plan, bank or broker is ever on the phone.

Step 1Phone
You ask “How much did I spend on food this month?”
Step 2Tunnel
The message travels through Tailscale to Moneo on your computer.
Step 3Moneo
Moneo reads your own records and calls the AI with the ChatGPT sign-in or API key saved on the computer.
Step 4Phone
The reply streams back. If the phone locks, the computer finishes the reply and saves it.
EverywhereDatabase
Edits, accepted cards and syncs land in the same database, so every screen has them. Pressing Sync on the phone runs the sync on the computer.

The three sign-ins

Connected once, on the computer

Each service sends the browser back to one fixed address on the computer. The laptop's addresses didn't change, so none of these flows did.

Without a guard

A dead page and a half-made connection

The phone would try to return to its own localhost, which has no Moneo on it.

With the guard

A clear message, and nothing started

Moneo recognizes a *.ts.net request and says to open Moneo on the computer for that one step.

Who can get in

No password, so the network has to be the lock

Moneo signs its one owner in automatically. That is only safe if nobody else can reach it.

Layer 1

Your devices only

Serve answers devices on your Tailscale network, so keep that network to yourself. There is no public address to find or attack.

Layer 2

Public traffic refused

If Tailscale Funnel is ever turned on, it marks each request. Moneo's proxy refuses those before the automatic sign-in.

Layer 3

Local services stay local

Moneo, the database and storage still bind to 127.0.0.1. Other devices on your Wi-Fi can't open them.

What Tailscale can and can't see

  • The tunnel is encrypted between your own devices
  • HTTPS ends on your computer, not on a server in between
  • The address appears in public certificate logs; no data does

What this leaves to you

  • Everyone on your Tailscale network gets straight in, so don't add other people
  • A lost phone has access until you remove it in Tailscale
  • Keep a screen lock on your devices

If you never turn it on

Nothing else changes

The feature had to be invisible to anyone who only uses Moneo on their computer.

Added for everyone

Small and measured

  • One card in Settings, loaded separately so it can't hold up the page
  • A Tailscale status check on Settings only: about 100 ms when installed
  • One header check per request in the proxy
Not added

Nothing running in the background

  • No Tailscale requirement
  • No background jobs, new ports or database changes
  • No new code in the browser for the QR code; it's drawn on the server
Fixed along the way

Chat attachments go through Moneo

They used to open from storage on 127.0.0.1, which only exists on the computer. Now they're served by the app, on every device.
