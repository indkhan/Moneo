What it does

Everything about your money, in one private place

Ask

Ask, and it does the work

“Find where I lent money to Bilal and add it.” Moneo searches your transactions, finds the payment and gives you a card to record the loan. “Buy €900 of MSCI World.” It prepares the order on Scalable and shows you exactly what it will cost.

  • Nothing changes until you tap the card
  • Uses your ChatGPT plan or your own API key
  • You choose which data the AI may see

Call

Call Moneo and ask several things at once

Tap Call Moneo and just talk. Ask three questions in one breath and Moneo works on all of them in parallel, telling you each answer as it lands. After the call you get a summary, every answer, and any cards to accept.

  • Interrupt, stop a task, or type instead
  • Works from your phone too

Artifacts

Build your own pages and reports

Describe the page you want, like a monthly money story, a subscription radar or a goals tracker, and Moneo builds it from your data. Keep it, refine it in chat, and pin it to your Home as a card.

  • Always shows your latest numbers
  • Runs in a sandbox with no internet access

Your Home

Widgets you design yourself

“Add a card that tracks my eating out against my budget.” Moneo turns any artifact into a Home widget that sits next to the built-in ones: net worth, budgets, goals and what's coming up. Arrange Home the way you want it.

  • Small, medium or large
  • Updates with your data every time you open Home

Wealth

Your net worth, traced

Bank accounts, your Scalable Capital portfolio with every holding and its gains, money friends owe you, and assets and debts you add yourself: one number with a year of history, and where every change came from.

  • Bank sync through Enable Banking (Europe)
  • Or import CSV and Excel statements, with undo

Plan

Goals, budgets and what's coming

Set goals like a house deposit or a trip, and Moneo tells you when you'll get there at your monthly plan. It finds your recurring payments, forecasts your cash and tracks your budgets.

  • Earmark savings for each goal
  • Try “what if” scenarios safely

Phone

On your phone too, still private

Turn on phone access and open Moneo from your own phone and tablet over Tailscale, a free private network for your devices. Add it to your home screen like an app. Your data stays on your computer.

Get it

Two ways to run it

Same app, same features. Changes to the code reach both.

Download the app

For everyone. Install it and open it: no Docker, Node.js or terminal. Updates come from Settings → App updates.

  1. Download Moneo for your computer below.
  2. Open it. The first start sets up your private database (about a minute).
  3. Add your AI in Settings, then import a statement or connect your bank.
↓ Download

Run from the source

For developers who want to read or change the code. Needs Git, Node.js 22 or newer and Docker.

git clone https://github.com/indkhan/Moneo.git
cd Moneo
npm ci
npm run setup
npm run build
npm start

Your computer may warn you the first time

The installers aren't code-signed yet, so your system doesn't recognise the publisher. You can check each file against SHA256SUMS.txt in the release.

  • Windows: if “Windows protected your PC” appears, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  • macOS: right-click Moneo in Applications, choose Open, then Open again.
  • Linux AppImage: make it executable (chmod +x Moneo-*.AppImage), then run it.

Privacy

Built so your data stays yours

No account, no cloud

Moneo runs on your computer. There is no Moneo server and no sign-up.

Only what you connect

Data goes only to your AI provider, and your bank or Scalable if you connect them. No analytics, no tracking.

Numbers come from code

Money is stored exactly, in its currency. The AI explains and proposes; it never quietly changes your data.

Nothing is lost

Imports and changes can be undone, and the desktop app backs up your data every day.

FAQ

Questions

Is Moneo free?

Yes. Moneo is free and open source under the MIT license. The AI uses your own ChatGPT plan or an API key, so any AI costs are between you and your provider.

Does any of my financial data go online?

Only to services you connect yourself: your AI provider when you ask a question, Enable Banking to sync your bank, and Scalable Capital for investments. Everything else stays in the database on your computer.

What's the difference between the app and running from source?

None in features. The app bundles everything, the database included, so you just install it. Running from source is for people who want to change the code; it uses Docker for the database.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Turn on phone access in Settings and connect your phone with Tailscale. Moneo stays on your computer; your phone opens it from there.

How do updates work?

In the app, open Settings → App updates and choose Check for updates. On Windows and Linux (AppImage) Moneo installs the update and restarts with your data. On a Mac it opens the download. From source: git pull, npm ci, npm run setup, npm run build, then npm start.

Which banks work?

Banks available through Enable Banking, which covers many banks across Europe. Any bank works through statement import.
