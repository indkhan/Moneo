Only what's recorded

Moneo counts what it has evidence of. Money at another bank or cash at home counts once you add it.

Never a silent guess

An unknown input is left out and named. If two balances conflict, Moneo uses the most recently recorded one and tells you.

History stays

Own transfers

Moving your own money doesn't change what you own

The two sides of a transfer between your tracked accounts often land on different days. Money in transit counts as yours, once.

How the sides are matched

Opposite amounts, same currency, up to 3 days apart, in accounts proven yours by IBAN or the bank's own wording. Exactly one candidate.

If it isn't clear

Two possible matches, or a side that doesn't arrive within the window, stay listed as unresolved on Wealth. Moneo doesn't guess.

Other banks, fees, currency

A transfer to a bank Moneo doesn't track leaves net worth on its own date. Fees and exchange-rate differences are real changes.

Life cycle

Each thing you own counts only while you own it

Past days use the state that was true then. Undoing an addition is the deliberate exception.

What happensEffectHow Moneo treats it
You sell, then rebuy laterShares count as zero in betweenThe sale cash counts in between, and the shares don't, so nothing counts twice.
You undo adding somethingAs if never addedIt disappears from past days too. Removing a real asset only counts from its removal date.
You link an asset to an accountCounted onceThe account's balance takes over from the link's date, and the old value stops.
You lend moneyNo changeThe money leaves your bank and appears as "Lent to" on the same day.
You get repaidRepaid amount: no changeEach repayment is tied to one bank payment and dated by it. A payment can't settle more than its amount, a cancelled one can't be used, and a bank correction or undo asks you again.
You withdraw cashNo change once acceptedA card offers to add it to your Cash account. Until you accept, it counts as money that left. After spending, you update the balance whenever you like.
You archive an accountDepends on your answerIt stays in past days until its archive date. If it holds money, Moneo first asks: moved to another account, taken as cash, or gone.
You add a bank or itemIn the total, not a gainThis month's change shows it apart as newly added.

Staying up to date

Old numbers first, fresh numbers when they land

Opening Moneo never waits on a bank. The page shows what's saved, then updates by itself once a sync finishes.

When you open it

Sync on open and return

Scalable at most every 15 minutes, and each bank at most hourly, because you're present.

In the background

Hourly job, about once a day each

Catches up after the computer was asleep. Scalable runs if its values aren't from today.

Per connection

Syncs don't clash

Each connection is claimed in the database, one at a time. An older result can't overwrite a newer one, and a renewed login token can't be used twice.

Bank data you can trust

  • The bank's balance is saved even when no new transaction arrives
  • Banks without transaction IDs compare how many identical rows the bank and Moneo each have, in a period both fully cover, so repeats aren't re-added and real second payments aren't lost
  • Unclear overlaps go to review instead of being guessed

A sync only counts when it landed

  • Reaching the bank doesn't count as success until the rows are in your ledger
  • A failed import never shows as a successful sync
  • A request that fails before reaching the bank is reported too

Trust

The numbers keep checking each other

Whenever the numbers are read, Moneo compares independent records. Each piece of evidence is one entry, until a correction fixes it.

Holdings, crypto and cash add up to Scalable's own total.

History meets reality

The last calculated day is compared with the first real sync.

Transfers pair up

Every own transfer has one other side, or is shown as unresolved.

Imports landed

A failed import can't publish a successful status.

Guaranteed by automated tests
  • A matched transfer between tracked accounts is counted once on every day
  • A returned withdrawal changes nothing
  • Importing the same row twice leaves every balance agreeing
  • With one currency, today's point equals the headline
  • Home, Wealth and chat agree on the same data
  • A loan and its repaid amount don't change net worth
Deliberately not alarms
  • Negative balances: an overdraft or a fee can be real
  • Exact match with Scalable's returns: its periods and rounding differ
  • A transfer taking longer than 3 days: weekends and reversals happen, so it stays listed as unresolved, without an alarm

Limits

What Moneo can't know, and where to read the code

Better to say this plainly than to imply a precision the records don't have.

Known limits

  • Money you never recorded isn't counted, and purchases count as spent even if they keep their value
  • Cash you spend only changes when you update the balance
  • Moneo relies on the bank's data, so it can't see a bank mistake that still adds up
  • Calculated days use the prices Scalable provides, monthly further back, in euros only; otherwise the chart starts at the first sync
  • The graph shows one display currency for now, while the headline converts (#158)
  • A cash machine card is offered for 30 days; older cash is yours to set as a balance

Takeaway

One number, built from your records, on every screen.

Honest about the past

Calm about moving money
