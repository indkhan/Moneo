Proposed system

Six stages. One traceable verdict.

Transaction type never becomes a spending category by itself. It constrains possible answers; purpose and counterparty explain intent.

Preserve evidence

Keep title, bank category, transfer purpose, sender, recipient, transaction type, references, amount direction, and raw CSV.

Normalize text

Case-fold, Unicode-normalize, remove bank boilerplate, isolate words and phrases, retain original values.

Resolve counterparty

Turn changing statement strings into stable entities without confusing marketplaces with underlying merchants.

Set financial role

Regular spend, income, internal transfer, refund, fee, or cash. This is not the budget category.

Apply evidence

User rules first. Then structured signals, merchant knowledge, bank mappings, purpose/title phrases, and agreement checks.

Decide or abstain

High assigns. Medium suggests but needs review. Low remains uncategorized. Save method, evidence, and classifier version.

Shippable sequence

Accuracy gate before category code.

Current import risks can change transaction identity and evidence. Fixing them first prevents rules attaching to the wrong account or disappearing after re-import.

Gate 0

Prove imports

  • Account separation
  • Malformed-row recovery
  • Status and transfer-purpose preservation
  • Deletion provenance
  • Real Commerzbank and Revolut fixtures
Phase 1

Category foundation

  • Versioned two-level taxonomy
  • Separate category assignments
  • Financial roles

  • Migration-safe IndexedDB stores
Phase 2

Local automation

  • Evidence extractor
  • Counterparty normalization
  • High-precision German rule pack

Personal learning

  • Coverage and correction metrics
Decision 02

Should custom categories ship immediately?

Recommendation: built-in two-level taxonomy first, but allow custom subcategories under fixed top-level groups. This gives personal control without breaking reports and future budgets.
